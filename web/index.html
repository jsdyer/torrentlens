      Metadata, not a safety verdict

      A filename ending in .exe or a suspicious path deserves attention. A clean-looking file list cannot establish that the content is safe or that it matches its name. Hashes identify the torrent metadata; they do not certify the publisher.

      Why a magnet has fewer details

      Most magnet links carry an infohash, a display name and tracker hints. The actual file list requires separate metadata. This inspector does not contact peers, resolve magnets, or download content.

      Privacy by design

      Inspection runs in a local worker. Files, links and reports are not saved unless you download a report. Clear removes the current input. The shared CoolCode counter records aggregate page opens, never your inspection data.
